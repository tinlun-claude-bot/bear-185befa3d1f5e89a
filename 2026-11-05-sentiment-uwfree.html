How it is counted

Each input is ranked against a frozen record of its own past that ends in 2022: the record's median day scores 0. Inputs in a category count equally; a category counts if half its inputs read. A missing category adds 0 and the weights do not shift. Under 60 of 100 read, or Bear, Trend or Fear unread: no read. History can add one vote of size 1 to News on the first morning after a scheduled event resolves, by script: at least 15 separate events and an up share at least 15 points from all days.

What it can and cannot say

Says where nine kinds of evidence stand this morning. Does not say the close, odds or size, and gives no reason to add risk during a bear signal. No test shows it predicts anything; every weight and line is an untested opinion.
